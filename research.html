Research

Conservation biology, ocean resilience, responses to climate change, and marine connectivity.

Projects

  1. 2025–now

    Recovery and recolonization of the sunflower sea star

    UCLA, Barber Lab · California coast

    My dissertation combines environmental DNA surveys of the California coast with larval particle tracking on a 32-year MOM6 Northeast Pacific ocean hindcast. Across three papers, I test whether larvae from surviving northern populations can reach southern California, how that connectivity shifts with ENSO, the Pacific Decadal Oscillation, and upwelling, and which sites are the strongest candidates for reintroduction.

    The work is supported by the Association of Zoos & Aquariums' Saving Animals From Extinction (SAFE) sunflower sea star program. I also built Pycno Tracker, a public map of sunflower sea star sightings from iNaturalist and GBIF alongside our sampling sites.

  2. 2025–2026

    Marine megafauna and coral reef monitoring

    NSF International Research Experiences for Students · Costa Rica

    With Dr. Laura May-Collado (University of Vermont) and the University of Costa Rica, I monitored marine megafauna and coral reefs using acoustic tagging, SCUBA surveys, passive acoustics, and coral gardening. I returned in 2026 as a field teaching assistant.

  3. 2024–now

    Characterizing coral reef biodiversity

    Love the Oceans · Guinjata Bay, Mozambique

    I'm assessing coral cover, fish diversity, and the effects of fishing practices to inform the creation of a locally managed marine protected area in southern Mozambique.

  4. 2024–now

    Using environmental DNA to assess biodiversity and connectivity

    ARMS Restore, Reef Doctor & IH.SM · Ranobe Bay, Madagascar

    With Dr. Aaron Hartmann (Harvard) and partners at the Institut Halieutique et des Sciences Marines, I use eDNA metabarcoding to survey fish communities and ecological connectivity across coral reef, mangrove, and seagrass habitats, informing artificial reef restoration in southwestern Madagascar.

  5. 2021–2023

    Damselfish, nitrate pollution, and coral growth

    UCSB, Stier Lab (Ocean Recoveries Lab) · Mo'orea, French Polynesia

    I assessed how the symbiotic relationship between humbug damselfish and their host corals changes across a nitrate pollution gradient.

  6. 2022

    Coral regeneration and growth trade-offs

    UCSB, Stier Lab (Ocean Recoveries Lab) · Mo'orea, French Polynesia

    I investigated energetic trade-offs between coral growth, tissue regeneration, and survival after physical wounding.

  7. 2021–2022

    Insect community ecology

    UCSB, Young Lab · Sierra Nevada, California

    I analyzed how rising lake temperatures affect insect emergence patterns in Sierra Nevada mountain lakes.

  8. 2019–2020

    Building STEM belonging in young girls

    UCSB Curie-osity Project, Gevirtz School of Education

    We designed and tested activities to improve memory retention, participation, and interest in science among a cohort of elementary school girls.
