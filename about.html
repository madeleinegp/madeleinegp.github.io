About

First-generation scientist, diver, and Southern California native.

I'm a proud first-generation student, born and raised in Southern California. I fell in love with marine science in 2016 as an Education Volunteer at the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach.

That path has since taken me from coral reefs in French Polynesia, Madagascar, Mozambique, and Costa Rica back home to California's kelp forests, where I now study how the sunflower sea star might recover. Along the way I've been an AAUS scientific diver, a field teaching assistant, and a mentor to students from high school through university.

Outside the lab I'm a yoga enthusiast, on a mission to visit every U.S. national park, and happiest underwater. I speak English and Spanish natively and am working on my French.
