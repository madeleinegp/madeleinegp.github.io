About

I'm a proud first-generation student, born and raised in Southern California. I fell in love with marine science in 2016 as an Education Volunteer at the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach.

I hold a deep appreciation for education. Attending university at UC Santa Barbara was a truly profound chapter of my life, one that broadened my world and shaped much of who I am today.

During my undergraduate studies, I received my open water scuba certification in 2022 and became an AAUS scientific diver in 2023. Around the same time, I added Environmental Studies as a second major, which is where I started to love the human dimensions of marine ecology and conservation.

In 2023, I moved to Los Angeles to begin graduate school at UCLA. Beyond the research, these years have been about curiosity, community, and saying yes to new places and experiences, both above and below the water.

Outside of science, I'm a yoga enthusiast and on a mission to visit every U.S. national park.
